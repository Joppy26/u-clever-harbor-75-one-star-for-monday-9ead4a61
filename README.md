Entry for the [One Star for Monday](https://make.fun/bounty/u-clever-harbor-75-one-star-for-monday-9ead4a61) competition on make.fun.

## Entry

> One star. The waiter sat me at a table called Monday, served cold coffee, then charged me my whole weekend.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the review as a single line of plain text to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `/output/entry.txt` when the variable is unset), within the gate of 1–180 characters and at most 25 words.
